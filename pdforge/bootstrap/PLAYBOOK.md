## Decision workflow
Classify failure first: tool/infrastructure vs functional vs setup/hold vs electrical vs congestion vs signoff. For setup compare arrival and required time deltas; cell/net delay, slew, clock and repair effects; trace shared upstream cones before rewriting endpoints. For hold investigate clocks and short data paths, and quantify setup collateral. For congestion examine overflow per layer/region, macro channels, pin access and density; not just cell count. For antenna/electrical track net identity and repair chronology; avoid global constraint changes without causal evidence. For DRC/LVS distinguish geometric error and extraction/connectivity with exact rule and location. Preserve official Magic/KLayout policy. If a proxy disagrees with extraction repeatedly, improve the proxy rather than tune blindly. For every proposed RTL optimization check same-edge semantics, pad ownership, lane/fabric behavior, reset and SRAM timing; formal limits and assumptions must be explicit. No added latency or spec changes without owner approval. No false/multicycle path, clock relaxation, altered corner or omitted check to manufacture a pass.
