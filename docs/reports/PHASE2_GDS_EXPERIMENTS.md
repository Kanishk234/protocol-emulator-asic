# Phase 2 fit and timing experiments

**Status checked:** 2026-10-01 (America/Chicago)
**Candidate under test:** `spike/r4-floorplan` at `3393eea9a58c5cad9077cc360a8515d0e5ec8284`
**Candidate shape:** 2 lanes, 4 pin units, U0 full; 20 ns clock; 56% placement density

This report tracks the experiments intended to get a complete Phase 2 hardening without lowering the 20 ns clock target or removing UART, SPI, I2C, or other implemented protocol behavior. The 2-lane/4-unit shape is the current protocol-floor candidate under test; these measurements do not approve the final hardware counts. The R4 branch uses D-056's 0.10 ns hold uncertainty for branch measurement (setup remains 0.25 ns); adopting that setting on `main` remains conditional on full hold and gate-level evidence. See [Phase 2 exit criteria](../design/phases/PHASE2_RTL_CORE.md) and [the related decisions](../DECISIONS.md).

## Constraints for the experiments

- Keep `CLOCK_PERIOD` at 20 ns and preserve the supported protocol behavior and firmware interface.
- Keep live pin reconfiguration legal. Under D-066, configuration latch-to-state paths remain timed; do not add a false-path exception.
- Change one hardware or flow variable per hardening and compare timing, global-route congestion, route runtime, and signoff together.
- Treat branch-only floor and hold settings as measurements, not approval to change final counts or move settings to `main`.
- Do not push hardware files while a hardening is running; the GDS path filter may cancel it.

## Run 36799356107 path and congestion diagnosis

- The slow-corner maximum path starts at U0's stored `idle` bit (`u_chip.g_unit[0].u_cfg.g_word[0].g_stored.g_bit[7].g_latch.q`) and ends at `u_chip.dropped[16]`, bit 0 of consumer C2 / L1.I0's DROPPED counter. In the candidate fabric map, C2 source 0 is U0.rx.
- The mapped path has 32 combinational standard cells and 12 fanout buffers between the latch output and counter D input. Its data arrival is 31.723 ns against 21.025 ns required, for −10.698 ns slack. The cell chain crosses U0's pin/drive and BITSYNC logic, the receive-token load path, and C2's tap/drop logic. This is a same-cycle live configuration-to-state path; keep it timed under D-066. The counter is the endpoint, but the bulk of the path is upstream logic depth and buffering, not the 8-bit counter increment.
- The archived GRT output had only aggregate layer totals. GRT-only replay [36899340155](https://github.com/Kanishk234/protocol-emulator-asic/actions/runs/36899340155) saved the missing bin report and reproduced 821 overflow. It identifies a broad east-west congestion pattern with a heavier x=600–700 µm band, rather than a single localized blockage. The bin report labels regions by horizontal/vertical direction, not metal layer; the per-layer breakdown comes from the GRT log. Its source annotations include lane, pin-unit, fabric, and clock-tree nets; those are nets crossing congested bins, not proven causes.
- Next routing step: run full LibreLane hardening with `GRT_ADJUSTMENT=0.12` applied through `--override-config`, then compare detailed-route runtime, route DRC, LVS, antenna, timing, and precheck against the baseline. Keep this flow experiment separate from any timing RTL change.
